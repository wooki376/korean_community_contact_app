## Why This Project Exists

For years, the Korean Society (한인회) has been the only place holding a master contact list for the community's affiliated organizations (OKTA, the Senior Association, the Business Federation, and others). That list lived in a single Excel spreadsheet, kept and updated only by the Society.

In practice, this created two recurring problems:

1. **Constant one-off requests.** Whenever another organization's leader (단체장) needed contact details for a member or another group, they had to individually ask the Society for it, since there was no shared, accessible source. The Society ended up being the single point of contact for every request.
2. **Slow, bottlenecked updates.** Because only the Society held and maintained the spreadsheet, any change to someone's phone number, email, or role sat unreflected until it was manually re-collected and re-sent — often long after the change actually happened.

This app changes that model. Instead of routing every request through a single office, each organization or individual keeps their own entry current in a shared Google Sheet. The web app reads that sheet live, so any update shows up for everyone immediately, and anyone who needs contact info can look it up themselves instead of asking the Society directly.

The trade is simple and self-serve: submit your own information once, and in return you get fast, direct access to everyone else's — rather than waiting on a single bottlenecked keeper of the list.
